Why this credential cannot be verified

This credential was listed in the Zend Yellow Pages directory at zend-zce.com. Zend paused its legacy certification program in 2023 and decommissioned that directory. Perforce, which acquired Rogue Wave and now owns Zend, has confirmed in writing that it no longer holds records for certifications issued before 31 August 2023 and cannot verify or reissue any of them.

This applies to every holder of a pre-2023 Zend credential — it is not specific to this record. The original certificate is available for inspection on request, together with the Perforce correspondence confirming the above.
